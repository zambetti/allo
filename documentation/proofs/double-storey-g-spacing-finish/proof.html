Sampled horizontal gj gap: 8.3873 → 48.3873 font units (chosen → refined).

ss15 adds positive advance only for gj; every other pair is unchanged by ss15. The supplied corrections are +0 at weights 100–600, +8 at 700, +27 at 800 and +40 at 900. They use 40 × smoothstep over design-coordinate 580–900, rounded; design coordinates are not CSS weight labels. +40 is a conservative optical trial, not an automatically derived minimum gap. These sampled horizontal gaps are not exact Euclidean distances or a guarantee of comfortable spacing.

Fixed states: chosen = ss17 + ss19; refined = ss15 + ss17 + ss19; boundary = ss15 + ss16 + ss17 + ss19. ss17 applies B to every plain g advance; ss19 adds B before o / e / c, so B + B = C. ss18 (C on all g) and ss20 (C before o / e / c) stay off. Never ss17 + ss20: that would make 3B on round followers. Existing kerning stays active.

Current policy reduces g by B before a space and at text end. The boundary trial adds +B only when g is followed by the font’s space glyph, cancelling that reduction there; it does not widen the space glyph itself. Terminal g retains B in both columns (an advance change of −B). In the fixed sample, the first g precedes a space; the thin rule after the final g marks the text-run end, including its advance. This is a word-space comparison, not an adopted exception or a general word-boundary rule.

ss16 follows the glyph space, not a generic whitespace class. Encoded characters in scope: U+0020 SPACE. The fixed sample uses U+0020 SPACE.
